01 Role Project originator and computer-engineering lead Stage Ground prototype development

Plant health · Edge sensing · Space agriculture

PUALEI

PUALEI stands for Precision Unmanned Agroscience Leveraging Edge Intelligence. It is a student-led research project developing a low-power plant-monitoring and capillary-wick irrigation system for space and resilient agriculture.

The problem

Plant stress can begin before obvious visual damage appears. Remote and space-relevant environments also limit water, crew attention, connectivity, and opportunities to recover from failures.

The system direction

  • Plant and chamber environmental sensing
  • Embedded acquisition and control
  • Capillary-wick water delivery only
  • Onboard inference for early stress interpretation
  • Ground experiments with documented acceptance criteria

My contribution

I initiated the project concept, developed major parts of the system architecture and proposal, coordinate the interdisciplinary build, and translate the research goals into milestones, technical documentation, stakeholder materials, and testable work.

02 Role Prototype integration and discovery Stage Technical validation

Biosecurity · Contact acoustics · Edge inference

TRACE-CRB

TRACE-CRB is an early contact-acoustic and vibration-sensing prototype for coconut rhinoceros beetle inspection triage: listen before you dig.

The system

  • Piezo contact sensing and analog signal conditioning
  • Calibrated USB audio acquisition on Raspberry Pi
  • FPGA-assisted feature extraction
  • Touchscreen outputs: Inspect First, Follow Up, or Clear
  • Machine-learning experiments with labeled signal examples

The honest limitation

A small proof of concept is not field validation. The project still needs stronger labeled data, realistic negative examples, repeatable acquisition, and testing under noisy operating conditions before detection claims can be defended.

03 Role Research contributor Tools Python, Jupyter, FAISS, embeddings

Trustworthy AI · RAG security · Document processing

VENOM-RAG

VENOM-RAG is a cybersecurity-focused AI demonstration showing how poisoned PDFs and hidden text can influence retrieval-augmented generation systems.

Research workflow

  • PDF extraction and hidden-character inspection
  • Chunking, embeddings, and FAISS retrieval
  • Local model experiments
  • Comparison of clean and poisoned document behavior
  • Reproducible Conda and Jupyter setup

Current contribution

I am helping clean the project into a clear Jupyter demonstration, document Mac and Windows setup, and make the research reproducible enough for classmates to run and explain.

GRO Control Room

GRO Control Room grew from work with University of Hawaiʻi Government Relations. The platform was designed to monitor Hawaiʻi Legislature hearings, bills, testimony, and vote records for content relevant to the university.

Work represented

  • Transcript ingestion and indexing
  • Semantic search and retrieval-augmented generation
  • Keyword detection and bill-version comparison
  • Python, BigQuery, Apify, and Google Cloud workflows
  • FastAPI services deployed through Cloud Run

Why it belongs here

This project shows backend engineering, product framing, stakeholder communication, and the discipline required to translate legislative information into a usable operational workflow.
