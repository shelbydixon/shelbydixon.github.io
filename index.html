Why the background matters

Ten years of field operations changed how I see systems.

Greenhouses, hydroponics, soil production, maintenance, supplies, and partner coordination taught me that a system is not successful because the diagram looks clean. It succeeds when the workflow remains usable, the failure modes are understood, and the evidence is good enough to make the next decision.

That is the perspective I bring to AI pipelines, embedded prototypes, and research. I care about source verification, clear limits, human review, and systems that survive contact with the real environment.
